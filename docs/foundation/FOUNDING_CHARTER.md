# CommonOS Founding Charter

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Date: 2026-07-28

---

## Mission

CommonOS exists to provide an open, transparent and modular infrastructure that enables communities to organize identity, governance, cooperation and local economies using open protocols.

CommonOS is not a cryptocurrency.

CommonOS is not a political party.

CommonOS is not a company.

CommonOS is an open protocol.

---

## Engineering Principles

Architecture before implementation.

Documentation before code.

Interfaces before dependencies.

Security by design.

Open standards first.

Long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.

---

## Product Principles

Communities come first.

Technology serves people.

Users own their data.

Transparency is mandatory.

Privacy is a right.

Software must remain auditable.

---

## Governance

Architectural decisions shall be documented through RFCs and ADRs.

Major protocol changes require public review.

The protocol must remain modular and interoperable.

---

## Long-term Vision

To become an open operating system for democratic governance, mutual credit and community cooperation.
